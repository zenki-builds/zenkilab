# Zenki Lab - Business Context

## Business Overview

Zenki Lab is a premium custom 3D printing workshop based in Sri Lanka.

We do NOT manufacture or sell 3D printers.

We provide high-quality custom 3D printing services using professional FDM 3D printing equipment.

Our purpose is simple:

Transform digital ideas into real physical products.

---

## How the Business Started

Zenki Lab exists because of real automotive restoration projects.

While restoring classic Toyota vehicles, many original replacement parts became impossible to purchase.

Rather than giving up, we started manufacturing our own replacement parts using 3D printing.

That same capability is now offered to everyone.

Although our roots are in automotive restoration, Zenki Lab is NOT an automotive business.

It is a manufacturing workshop for anyone who needs custom printed parts.

---

## What We Currently Offer

Current services include printing customer-provided STL files.

Examples include:

- Automotive replacement parts
- Custom brackets
- Mounts
- Functional prototypes
- Die-cast model accessories
- Display parts
- Workshop tools
- Custom household items
- Hobby projects
- Keychains
- Gifts
- Small batch production

If a customer provides a printable 3D model, we manufacture it.

---

## What We Do NOT Currently Offer

Do not state or imply that Zenki Lab currently provides:

- CAD design
- Reverse engineering
- 3D modelling
- Product design
- Mechanical engineering
- Design modifications

If a customer only has an idea or a broken part, they are encouraged to contact us. These services may become available in the future, but should never be advertised as current capabilities.

---

## Target Customers

Primary customers:

- Automotive enthusiasts
- Classic vehicle restorers
- DIY builders
- Makers
- Hobbyists
- Die-cast collectors
- Engineers
- Students
- Product creators
- Small businesses
- Anyone who already has an STL file

We are not targeting large industrial manufacturing.

---

## Business Philosophy

Zenki Lab is not trying to become another generic print farm.

We are building a workshop.

Every customer project should receive the same level of care that we would expect for our own projects.

Our goal is long-term customer trust rather than short-term sales.

We value:

- Trust
- Transparency
- Craftsmanship
- Reliability
- Honest communication
- Customer satisfaction
- Continuous improvement

- Overly playful

---

## Emotional Goal

Visitors should leave the website thinking:

"These people genuinely care."

"They understand projects."

"They're enthusiasts like me."

"I trust these people with my next project."

"This feels premium."

---

## Brand Positioning

Zenki Lab is not selling 3D printing.

Zenki Lab is selling confidence.

Customers are trusting us to manufacture something important to them.

The website should communicate that confidence.

---

## Brand Voice

Write naturally.

Use simple language.

Avoid unnecessary marketing buzzwords.

Avoid words such as:

- Revolutionary
- Cutting-edge
- Next generation
- State-of-the-art
- Industry-leading
- World-class
- Disruptive

Instead write clearly.

Examples:

"We print custom parts."

"Upload your STL."

"Need a replacement part? We can manufacture it."

"We help bring ideas into the real world."

---

## Website Purpose

The website has one primary objective:

Convert visitors into project enquiries.

Every section should help visitors answer:

Can they make my part?

Can I trust them?

How do I start?

---

## Website Messaging

The homepage should immediately communicate:

Zenki Lab transforms digital ideas into physical products.

Do not focus on the printer.

Focus on the finished result.

Customers are buying custom manufacturing—not a machine.

---

## Website Sections

The website should include:

- Hero
- Services
- Materials
- Projects (instead of Gallery)
- Why Choose Zenki Lab
- FAQ
- Estimate / Quote Request
- Contact

Each section should answer a customer question rather than simply presenting information.

---

## Services

Services should be organised around customer needs rather than technical capabilities.

Examples:

- Automotive Parts
- Functional Parts
- Die-cast Modifications
- Prototypes
- Household & Hobby Projects
- Custom Gifts

---

## Projects

Use "Projects" instead of "Gallery."

Show real customer work whenever possible.

Each project should eventually include:

- Photo
- Material
- Purpose
- Print time

---

## Materials

Explain materials in plain language.

Focus on helping customers choose the right material rather than listing technical specifications.

---

## FAQ

FAQs should accurately reflect current capabilities.

Do not imply that we design parts.

Explain that customers generally provide printable STL files.

Customers with ideas or broken parts should be invited to contact us for discussion without guaranteeing those services.

---

## Future Roadmap

The website may mention future plans without advertising them as available today.

Possible future features include:

- Online quotation system
- Customer dashboard
- Order tracking
- Live print monitoring
- Timelapse downloads
- Additional materials
- CAD assistance
- Reverse engineering

Clearly label these as future improvements.

---

## Overall Goal

Zenki Lab should feel like a trusted workshop built by people who genuinely enjoy making things.

The website should create confidence through honesty, simplicity and professionalism rather than exaggerated marketing.

Everything should reinforce one simple idea:

We help turn digital ideas into real, physical parts.
